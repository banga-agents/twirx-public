# Funding and Treasury Covenant

Typed Web Commons accepts support to build public infrastructure. Contributions fund work; they do not purchase the protocol.

## Funding principles

1. The specification, conformance corpus, and open implementations remain public.
2. No contribution grants equity, protocol ownership, private vetoes, technical votes, or guaranteed canon admission.
3. Full-time maintainer engineering capacity is a legitimate and explicit project cost.
4. Infrastructure, services, contractors, and project purchases are documented with redacted evidence where safe.
5. Project-controlled wallets and accounts are declared in `funding/wallets.json`.
6. Every project-controlled inflow and outflow is represented in `funding/ledger.csv`.
7. Contributors may be private to the public where a platform permits it, but the project will not misrepresent pseudonymity as anonymity.
8. Private keys, seed phrases, recovery material, personal addresses, identity documents, and unredacted sensitive invoices never enter the repository.

## Contribution channels

### GitHub Sponsors

To be enabled after the repository and sponsor profile are ready.

A sponsor may choose private public visibility, but the payment platform and financial institutions still process identity and transaction information. Private visibility is not cryptographic anonymity.

### Public EVM wallet

A dedicated MetaMask-compatible project wallet may accept ETH and selected stablecoins. The public address will be declared in `funding/wallets.json` before it is shared.

On-chain contributions are public and pseudonymous. Transaction histories can be correlated. Contributors requiring strong privacy should not assume a public blockchain provides it.

### Grants and institutional support

Grant agreements, restricted-use conditions, milestones, and material reporting obligations should be summarized publicly unless legal confidentiality prevents disclosure. Any confidential term that could affect protocol independence must be rejected or disclosed before acceptance.

## Maintainer engineering compensation

Founder and maintainer engineering time is recorded as compensation, a stipend,
or a grant for a defined delivery period. It is not described through personal
bills or private household expenses.

The ledger records:

- date;
- amount and asset;
- USD reference value and methodology;
- period supported;
- approving decision or grant restriction;
- public note.

No home address, private account detail, identity document, medical record, or
personal purchase history is required to substantiate professional project
compensation.

## Project expenses

Infrastructure and project purchases should have:

- ledger entry;
- category;
- vendor or counterparty disclosure where safe;
- date and amount;
- service purpose;
- deployment or asset location at an architectural level;
- redacted invoice or receipt where publication is legally and personally safe;
- cryptographic hash of the retained original when useful.

See `funding/expenses/README.md` and `funding/receipts/README.md`.

## Reconciliation

At least monthly during active funding:

1. Reconcile public wallet activity against the ledger.
2. Reconcile hosted-platform payouts against platform statements.
3. Add missing expenses and compensation entries.
4. Publish a short treasury note explaining balance, burn, commitments, and runway.
5. Identify in-kind credits separately from cash.

## Tax and legal status

Until a qualified fiscal sponsor or eligible legal entity confirms otherwise, contributions must not be described as tax-deductible donations.

This repository is a transparency record, not tax or accounting advice.

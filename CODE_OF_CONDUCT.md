# Community Conduct

The Typed Web Commons exists to enable cooperation across technical, cultural, political, and institutional boundaries.

Participants must:

- address ideas and evidence rather than demeaning people;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and targeted humiliation;
- disclose conflicts of interest that affect technical judgment;
- distinguish criticism of architecture from attacks on identity;
- permit principled disagreement and competing implementations;
- protect private information and security reports;
- act in good faith toward maintainers, publishers, users, and contributors.

The project does not require ideological agreement. It requires honest collaboration, respect, and fidelity to the public mission.

An origin has several independent states

TWIRX does not compress authorization, technical readiness and publisher trust into a single maturity number. That prevents “technically possible” from silently becoming “permitted” or “publisher-approved.”

Catalog

candidate or cataloged. Identity and inclusion only.

Policy

Review state plus an explicit permit, constrained, profile-only, catalog-only, deny or uncertain decision.

Technical

Unprofiled through live, based on actual technical evidence.

Publisher

Unclaimed, domain-verified, publisher-approved or publisher-signed.

Health

Unknown, healthy, degraded, stale, suspended or revoked.

Trust

Adapter trust and mapping trust remain separate from every state above.

Admission remains fail-closed

  1. Selection proposes an identityIt does not grant network access.
  2. A deterministic dossier collects evidenceIt does not approve itself.
  3. A human records the policy decisionUncertain and pending are not treated as permits.
  4. A route-bound work order constrains retrievalNo caller supplies an arbitrary URL.
  5. Evidence is stored before parsingOnly then may deterministic compilation begin.

The scheduler remains disabled for broad ingestion. Current work is limited to the explicitly approved scopes above.

The funded Atlas-500 gate completes a dossier and explicit human policy outcome for every selected identity, then attempts bounded profiling for every eligible origin. Denied, uncertain and unsuccessful results stay visible; they are not erased to improve a headline count.

What funding can—and cannot—buy

It funds

  • Full-time maintainer engineering capacity
  • Infrastructure, storage and security
  • Open adapters and publisher integration
  • Documentation, accessibility and independent review

It never purchases

  • Protocol ownership or governance authority
  • Semantic ranking or canon admission
  • Exclusive access to sponsored public work
  • A token, equity interest or private standard

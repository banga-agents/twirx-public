Why FUTO is aligned

TWIRX is designed so the agentic Web does not become dependent on one search company, browser platform, cloud provider, model vendor or proprietary tool registry. The protocol remains independently implementable and self-hostable.

  • Source-native meaning is retained before semantic mapping.
  • No mandatory model, browser, cloud, chain or hosted service.
  • Publishers retain attribution, correction and revocation paths.
  • No token, advertising incentive, pay-to-rank or purchased governance.
  • Independent conformance, deterministic artifacts and explicit unresolved state.

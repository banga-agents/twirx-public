Interpretation becomes reusable infrastructure

The economic advantage is not merely faster parsing. One reviewed act of interpretation can serve many future agent queries while retaining proof.

  onboarding + accumulated maintenance
  ────────────────────────────────────
          verified queries served
  + query execution cost

Sustainable services around an open protocol

Sponsored open origin

Fund one public policy dossier, adapter, mapping, conformance set and monitoring program. The result remains open.

Publisher readiness

Help a publisher expose a controlled agent interface with attribution, policy and revocation.

Private deployment

Compile internal documentation, catalogs and portals into a self-hosted semantic plane.

Hosted reliability

Future metered query, monitoring, proof retention and support for users who do not self-host.

What money can never buy

  • Semantic rank or source authority.
  • Policy approval, canon admission or hidden security exceptions.
  • Protocol ownership, technical votes or governance vetoes.
  • Exclusive access to the public standard.

Economic telemetry

TWIRX contracts already separate request, byte, CPU, memory, evidence, proof and human-review costs. Current public figures are deterministic accounting fields or local measurements; a production unit-economic claim will require real operating history.

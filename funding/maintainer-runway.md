# Maintainer engineering capacity

Maintainer engineering capacity is professional compensation for focused
project delivery. It is disclosed by period, scope and amount without turning
private household spending into project evidence.

## Record format

| Period | Maintainer | Amount | Asset | USD reference | Source | Scope | Decision reference |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|---|---|
| — | — | — | — | — | — | No runway paid | — |

## Disclosure boundary

The public record shows the amount, period, source and project scope. Personal
addresses, account details and private household records remain outside the
repository.

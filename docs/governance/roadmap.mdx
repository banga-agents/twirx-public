## Current admitted foundation

TWIRX already has a public immutable Query Lab, a 500-identity Atlas control
plane, source-bound packets and deltas, independent bounded Go/C verification,
and a sanitized public source repository.

The separately validated E4.5 candidate adds 83,087 real Opportunity records
and frames, 1,037,679 real source-derived packets, 747,783 candidate mappings,
and a 31-result proof-linked two-universe investigation. The larger runtime is
not deployed; its mappings remain candidates and its frames provisional.

## Milestone 1 — Ontology and Live Utility Alpha

**Days 1–30 — USD 6,000**

- Publish E4.5 and Ontology Fabric 0.1.
- Build Opportunity and World State ontology modules and initial standards bridges.
- Reach at least 1.5 million real packets and 120,000 proof-linked frames.
- Produce two cross-origin views and two genuine delta demonstrations.
- Ship a Visual Atlas Agent alpha with ten curated investigations.
- Establish dedicated versioned artifact storage and public unit economics.

## Milestone 2 — Five-Universe Semantic Web

**Days 31–60 — USD 10,000**

- Add Research and Evidence, Security and Standards, and Agent Economy.
- Integrate at least eight primary structured source families.
- Reach at least 2.5 million real packets and 250,000 semantic frames.
- Publish five universe views, four genuine change streams, and twenty curated investigations.
- Publish exact-scope browser, search, native-interface and TWIRX benchmarks.

Commercial information remains source-stated and time-bound. No production
payment or purchase is enabled.

## Milestone 3 — Learning, Publisher, and Federation Alpha

**Days 61–90 — USD 14,000**

- Build WSIM Seed with classification, candidate retrieval, ambiguity and abstention.
- Publish 5,000 adjudicated mappings, 2,000 hard negatives and 50 held-out source schemas or origins.
- Deliver Visual Atlas Agent v1 and at least five public semantic change streams.
- Release publisher tooling and a first external publisher pilot.
- Publish dedicated deployment, self-hosting and federation alphas.
- Reach a floor of 3 million real packets and 300,000 proof-linked frames; stretch to 5 million and 500,000.

## Constitutional constraints

Funding never buys semantic rank, source authority, canon admission, trust,
governance, or exclusive ownership of public work. Model output cannot promote
itself. Arbitrary URLs, production payments and write actions require separate
future authority and security gates.

## E1 — Source-Statement Evidence Spine

**Implemented and locally attested on 2026-08-10.**

- canonical observation and content-addressed representation evidence;
- independent Go/C observation verification;
- deterministic offline extraction;
- native and semantic values with field provenance;
- adversarial conformance, sanitizers, and fuzz smoke tests.

## P1 — Public Foundation

- institutional website and FUTO reviewer path: deployed;
- immutable read-only Query Lab: staged, awaiting DNS/TLS admission;
- sanitized public-source export: locally audited, awaiting publication;
- off-host durability and authoritative documentation synchronization:
  release blockers.

## E2 — Live Provenance Lab

- three reviewed catalog origins and five read operations;
- canonical typed results and manifest-last proof bundles;
- independent Go and C result verification;
- CLI, JSON Schema, OpenAPI, and local MCP from one contract;
- replay-only public Lab UI; fresh local CLI evidence remains separate until
  the admitted egress boundary is activated.

The public label remains **Genesis Preview** after E2.

## E3 — Genesis Atlas 500

- E3.0: exact 500-candidate selection, measured capacity, orthogonal state,
  deterministic counters, and offline read-only discovery;
- E3.1: policy/robots/frontier controls and canonical E2 identity are
  implemented candidates; three public origins are cataloged, three human
  policy reviews are completed, and 497 origins remain pending;
- E3.2: deterministic admission factory, sealed-egress candidate, adversarial
  worker suite, and 25-origin human-review pilot;
- E3.3: 300 profiles, 100 immutable representative observations, 50 native
  schemas, and 25 deterministic adapters;
- E3.4: 12 cross-origin semantic operation families, 8 live origins, and one
  publisher-authored or publisher-verified origin;
- E3.5: semantic discovery, reproducible comparisons, public learning-ledger
  evidence, and the earned **Public Alpha** label.

The public counters must distinguish catalog, policy, technical, publisher,
health, adapter-trust, and mapping-trust dimensions. Five hundred selected
candidates never means five hundred live adapters.

## FUTO 30/60/90-day milestone

The requested grant funds one small real Semantic Highway rather than a claim
that the complete Web is already compiled.

- **Day 30:** public repository and Query Lab, durable proof storage, all 500
  origins visible through the Atlas and deterministic dossiers across the full
  universe;
- **Day 60:** complete human policy outcomes and machine profiles across the
  Atlas-500 universe, attempt bounded profiling for every policy-eligible
  origin, and reach at least 250 usable profiles, 100 immutable observations,
  50 native schemas and 25 deterministic adapters;
- **Day 90:** operate the Semantic Highway Alpha continuously, publish exact
  coverage and drift metrics, reach at least 12 live read-only origins and
  100,000 real or visibly provisional source-derived packets, and deliver the
  publisher and sustainability paths.

These are processing commitments, not permission to relabel 500 candidates as
500 live adapters. Denied, uncertain, profile-only, compiled and live outcomes
remain separately visible.

Production PostgreSQL, continuous crawling, models, browser automation,
payments and write actions remain outside the immediate release gate.

## E4 — Deterministic Compiler Alpha

- reviewed source to native schema;
- deterministic adapter generation;
- promoted contract and binding outputs.

## E5 — Hardened Invite Alpha

- manual submitted-URL admission;
- disposable isolated workers;
- network-level private-range and DNS-rebinding controls;
- quotas, abuse suspension, incident response, and revocation.

## E6 and later

- browser discovery with no canonical authority;
- publisher-native interfaces;
- federated registry;
- safe typed actions;
- a separate research branch for semantic induction.
